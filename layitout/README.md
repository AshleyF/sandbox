# Lay It Out

A one-page floor plan for figuring out where the furniture goes.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded. The room you’re working on stays in this browser, including the sizes, the finishes, and any photos. **Save this room** keeps a named copy there too, so you can open the bedroom again after switching to the bath.

## What you can do

- Set the room and the ceiling. **How to** at the top walks through the page, and it starts closed. Under Openings, add or remove doors, windows, and closets. Each one is a single line; click it to edit, or drag it along its wall. Widths use the same feet-and-inches or centimeters as the room.
- Add each piece with its real width, depth, and height, in feet and inches or in centimeters. **Rotate** and **Delete** sit on the piece itself. A photo is optional — it shows up on the plan and on the wall views so two brown dressers don't get mixed up.
- Drag on the graph-paper plan. Pieces snap to 6 inches, or to 5 cm, and to walls and each other when you get close. Scroll to zoom. Double-click, or press R, to rotate. The dark edge is the back (headboard, sofa back, the side of the desk that sits against the wall).
- **ft** and **cm** switch the whole plan: the room, the pieces, the grid, and the materials list. A saved room keeps its size when you switch.
- **Save this room** stores a copy under the room’s name. Open one from the list to bring it back, finishes and photos included. **Update saved room** writes over that copy. **Delete copy** drops only the copy. The button says Saved once that copy is stored.
- **Fit** places things so the door can open, the bed is on a wall, nightstands land beside it, and a chair lands at the desk.
- **Flow** does that, then prefers a few widely repeated feng shui habits: see the door from the bed without lying in line with it, a solid wall behind the headboard, the desk on a side wall so the door isn't at your back, and the closet front left clear. It is a layout aid, not a reading.
- **Walls** is the room from inside, one wall at a time, with heights. **Corners** is a dollhouse view you can turn. **Fit room** on the plan zooms back to the walls. It is separate from the Fit layout.
- Changing the room width or length keeps a piece that was against the east or south wall against that wall. Undo covers that edit, and a renamed room.
- The note under the plan is the one layout problem that matters most: an overlap, a blocked door swing, a bed off the wall, or a piece wider than the door.
- **Remodel** switches the same room into a bath job. The add list leads with a tub, toilet, and vanity. Tub walls show up once a tub is in the room. The side list adds up the materials from the sizes on the plan. Check off what you already have. Home Depot and Lowe's each appear once, as a search for the list. The prices are planning estimates.

The sample front bedroom is there so the tools aren't empty on a first visit. **Sample bath** is an 8′ by 10′ remodel. Replace the sizes with the real room.

## What this doesn't do

- It does not look products up from a photo. Measure the piece and type the size.
- The prices are planning estimates, not today's shelf price, and nothing here is an affiliate link or a store account.
- The suggester is a placer with rules, not a model that has seen the room. Drag anything it gets wrong.
- The corner view is a measured drawing, not a rendering of the finish or the light. Tile and a tub show up as the shapes on the plan.
