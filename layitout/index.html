Lay It Out

A room plan, and the list to remodel it.

How to use this
  1. Set the room size under Room. ft and cm only change how the numbers are shown.
  2. Under Openings, Add door, Add window, and Add closet put a new one on a wall. Remove is on that opening. Wall picks the side. From the left (or From the top on a side wall) is how far along the wall it sits. Hinge is which end a door swings from.
  3. Under In the room, add furniture, then drag it on the plan. Scroll to zoom. Double-click, or press R, to turn a piece. The dark edge is the back.
  4. Walls and Corners show the same room from inside. Arrange places what you’ve added.
  5. Save this room keeps a named copy in this browser. The room you’re editing stays here as you work.
