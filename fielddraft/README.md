# Field Draft

A one-page plan sheet for a builder who is sketching a building or a remodel.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded. The sheet stays in this browser.

## What you can do

- Set the outside width, length, and wall height. Put one door and one window on a wall. “From the left” is along the top and bottom walls. “From the top” is along the side walls.
- The roof pitch is rise per 12 inches of run. The sheet gives the angle, the percent, the rise, and the rafter length for a gable that spans either the width or the length.
- Stairs take a floor-to-floor rise and a tread. The sheet splits that into risers and a total run.
- Drainage takes a run and a drop and reports inches per foot and percent.

## What this doesn't do

- It does not know the building code for a state, a county, or a city. The stair note compares against a common residential habit: a riser of 7 3/4″ or less and a tread of 10″ or more. The adopted code is whatever the building department uses.
- It is a drafting aid, not a permit set. There is no seal, no scale bar that claims a printed scale, and no structural design.
